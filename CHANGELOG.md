# Changelog

All notable changes to Dry Run are listed here. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-16

First public prototype, built and published as a Claude artifact.

### Added
- Game builder: describe any problem or genre, then choose a difficulty (Casual, Realistic, Brutal), a length (6, 10 or 15 turns) and a simulation depth (Fast or Deep).
- Eight example cartridges: Startup Manager, Football Manager, The Pay Rise, Restaurant Manager, Winter Crisis, Launch a Podcast, New City and Ship the App.
- Claude-generated worlds: competing stats with lose conditions, characters with agendas, a measurable goal and a fitting time unit.
- d20 resolution with three risk levels, a difficulty modifier, five outcome tiers and the odds shown on every choice.
- Custom moves: write your own move and choose how bold it is.
- Live stat meters with change badges, goal progress, cast list and decision log.
- End-of-game result, score and grade, plus a coach's debrief: best and weakest move, decision style, lessons and a real-world plan.
- Replay the same world with fresh dice, and copy the debrief.
- Offline demo, *Seed Stage*, a five-month startup run that needs no Claude access.
- Autosave and resume, and a mobile-first layout.
